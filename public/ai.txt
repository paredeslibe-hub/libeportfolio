### Project 3: Portal de Créditos — Worker Financial Data Platform
Role: UX/UI Designer
Client: Confidential (Mexico / Chile)
Industry: Fintech / HR Tech / Lending
Timeline: 3 months

SITUATION:
A B2B fintech company aggregated employment and salary data from partner employers to offer personalized credit products directly to workers. The existing worker-facing portal had confusing navigation, an opaque eligibility process, and a 65% form abandonment rate on credit applications. Customer support was being overwhelmed by avoidable UX-related queries.

TASK:
Redesign the worker-facing credit portal to: (1) simplify and shorten the application flow, (2) communicate credit eligibility clearly and in real-time, (3) reduce the volume of support tickets generated by interface confusion, and (4) deliver a mobile-responsive experience for a workforce that primarily accessed the platform via smartphones.

ACTION:
- Conducted a full UX audit: identified 14 heuristic violations, 3 classified as critical blockers
- Ran card-sorting sessions (n=12) and tree-testing to restructure the information architecture
- Reduced the credit application form from 24 fields on a single page to 9 progressive-disclosure screens
- Designed a real-time eligibility feedback component showing users exactly where they stood in the approval process
- Created a mobile-first responsive design system optimized for low-bandwidth connections
- Coordinated with the dev team to implement analytics events on each form step for ongoing optimization

RESULT:
- Form completion rate increased from 35% to 71% (+103% improvement)
- Customer support tickets related to UX confusion decreased by 45% in the 60 days post-launch
- Average session duration decreased by 28% (positive signal: users found information faster)
- Time-to-complete a credit application reduced from 18 minutes to 6 minutes
- Full design delivery and dev handoff completed within the 3-month timeline
